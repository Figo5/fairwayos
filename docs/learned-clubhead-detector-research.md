# Author-labeled clubhead detector research

A single YOLO11n fine-tune on independently authored ClubheadDB boxes did not improve the current Tiger 2019 Masters route. The candidate is rejected; no model, default tracker or selected export was replaced. The local [results report](~/fairway-os-runs/tiger-learned-head-cycle/REPORT.md) and [clip index](~/fairway-os-runs/tiger-learned-head-cycle/CLIPS.md) contain the actual evidence and four additional official Masters trims processed through the existing broadcast workflow.

`ghostcaddie/demo/clubhead_data.py` assembles byte-bound author JPEGs and one-class YOLO labels from a fixed plan, annotations and acquisition receipt. It requires explicit source/frame hashes and sampled alignment assertions bound to source, ordered author rows and ordered frame hashes. It rejects malformed boxes, duplicate/missing rows and cross-split exact-byte or declared-identity overlap. All-null author rows become negative labels; an absent row never becomes a negative. Normalized public-media IDs preserve case-sensitive YouTube identifiers. Atomic staging avoids half-published datasets. Review assertions do not prove label correctness, human verification, physical head-core identity or perceptual deduplication.

Two acquired training sources had gross temporal mismatch and were excluded whole; three planned Reddit sources were unavailable, with no replacement. Eight training sources supplied 520 frames and three validation sources supplied 441. Training followed one frozen 30-epoch maximum protocol with preset patience 10; it stopped after 18 epochs, selecting epoch 8 by validation AP50–95. The original loaded source was not hashed at process start: an epoch-4 snapshot and unchanged training-function comparison provide partial provenance only. Final evaluation source/dependencies were frozen before test unsealing, and the immutable training manifest was not rebuilt during later assembler hardening.

`tools/clubhead_detector_experiment.py` performs opt-in training and guarded one-shot evaluation. The four test source groups were fixed before alignment sampling and remain all included. Checkpoint, protocol, source/dependency hashes and test selection are bound before inference. Confidence-ranked one-to-one author-box matching counts duplicate and wrong-box predictions as false positives; a wrong box also leaves a miss. Reports include pooled/per-source AP, a source-level bootstrap and validation-selection disclosure. Four test sources and partial AI alignment review do not establish statistical or production generalization. ClubheadDB declares CC-BY-NC-4.0; this is local research.

The fixed test produced AP50 0.6974, AP50–95 0.3795, precision 0.6148 and recall 0.8158 across 597 frames at confidence 0.25 and matching IoU 0.50. Source AP50 spans 0.2714–0.9011. The backlit driver is a weakness, and 7/16 explicit-negative frames have detections. Validation AP50 is 0.6314 and AP50–95 0.3402; validation selected the checkpoint.

`tools/tiger_learned_head_probe.py` replays that frozen one-class model on the existing 1,078 checksum-bound native-resolution Tiger cache crops, with no reference coordinates or labels read by inference. Only one above-threshold box permits a center proposal. Seed frames remain operator anchors, absent/ambiguous detections remain missing, and cuts suppress current/future head proposals. Box centers and network confidence are not physical-core or calibrated identity evidence. Putter inference is outside the training domain.

On the already inspected sparse AI references the learned route scores 17 correct, 10 wrong and 65 missing /92. The seven full swings score 17/9/12 /38, compared with the selected route's 24/0/14. Tree branches are repeatedly mistaken for clubheads and no correct putter sample is scored. All twelve selected videos and the selected prediction file are byte-identical to the previous delivery. Ball blocks remain copied unchanged. These are development diagnostics, not held-out gains or all-frame accuracy.

The original replay required a seed-note metadata adapter and a status adapter without changing pixels or numerical predictions. The future runner accepts descriptive seed notes and emits the shared legacy automatic-observation status with `learned_bbox_center` as its actual method. This method is now admitted by the benchmark allowlist; metric rules are unchanged. Post-evaluation runner fixes add strict missing-seed handling, seed/cut regression tests and output-hash receipts. Archived original snapshots are authoritative for reproducing the recorded run; the live repaired probe intentionally has a different hash. The frozen benchmark's old scope prose is less complete than its method allowlist.
